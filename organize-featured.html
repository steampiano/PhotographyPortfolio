Organize Featured Order

A local-only authoring tool — not part of the live site.

Add a photo

Upload a photo to add it to the site. It's saved into your photos, thumbnails are generated, and (if checked below) it's featured — then it appears in the list to drag into position.

Uploading needs the helper server. Launch this tool with the “Organize Featured.command” file (not plain python3 -m http.server).
